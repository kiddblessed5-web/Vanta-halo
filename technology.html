Four motors, no shafts

Each wheel has its own compact motor, so torque can be placed exactly where grip is. Software adjusts it ten thousand times a second. On a wet road it feels like traction control that never has to intervene.

03Aerodynamics

Air as a tool

The flat floor and active shutters keep drag at 0.21 for range. Above 120 km/h the rear wing lifts through three positions. At 250 km/h it presses the car down with 380 kg of force and the airbrake position shortens braking distances.
