From clay to carbon: the Halo timeline

Heritage · 7 min read

Every car has a ghost fleet of the ones that did not happen. The Halo's belongs to a quarter-scale clay model, a mule with a borrowed steering wheel and a prototype that arrived with its roof upside down.

The clay set the proportions: a long roofline, a short tail and a stance wide enough to look planted from every angle. We have tried to change it a dozen times, and every time the car got worse.

The roofline is the only thing we never redesigned.

The mule, Prima, proved the four-motor layout, then proved its limits by losing a roof panel on the first test day. The carbon monocoque that followed weighs 96 kg and is stiffer than anything we could have made out of steel.

The concept shown in 2025 was lit from one side and left in the dark. That choice has shaped how we show every Halo since.
