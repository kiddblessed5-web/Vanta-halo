About VANTA

We started with a sketch of a roofline and an argument that a great car does not need to shout.

Our story

Drawn in the dark

VANTA began in 2019 in a rented garage in Nairobi. The first Halo was a clay model lit from one side, and the idea was simple: build a grand tourer that disappears into the night and feels lighter than it is.

Seven years later, that idea has a carbon tub, four motors and a solid-state battery. It also has a home: an atelier in Nairobi where every Halo is finished by hand.

Evolution

Six generations of one idea

Every Halo starts from the same sentence: make the car feel lighter than it is. Seven years of clay, carbon and code later, it still does.

  • 2019

    Clay

    A single sheet of paper and one promise: the longest roofline we could fit over four wheels. Quarter-scale clay, shaped by hand in a rented garage in Nairobi.

  • 2020

    Prima

    The first running mule. Four motors, a borrowed steering wheel and a roof that did not survive the first test day.

  • 2022

    Monocoque

    The carbon tub. One piece, 96 kg, cured in our own atelier. It set the proportions every later car had to keep.

  • 2023

    Solid state

    Cells that accept 320 kW and take 10 to 80 per cent in 14 minutes, without the heat that normally limits it.

  • 2025

    Halo concept

    Shown in the dark with the lights off. Nobody asked about the specifications for ten minutes.

  • 2026

    Production

    Orders open. Deliveries begin in Nairobi, then Mombasa.
