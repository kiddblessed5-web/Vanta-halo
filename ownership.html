Questions

Good to know

Can I get a Halo registered in my own name or a company's?
Yes. We handle registration, number plates and insurance introductions for individuals and companies.
Is there import duty to pay?
Duty and VAT are included in every price we show. Taxes on electric vehicles in Kenya can change, so your final quote confirms the current position.
How long does delivery take?
In-stock cars can be delivered within a week of payment. Cars on the water arrive in three to six weeks. A built-to-order car takes about four months.
What happens if the battery degrades?
The battery carries an eight year or 200,000 km warranty covering capacity loss below 70 per cent.
Can I trade in my current car?
Yes. Bring it to your test drive and we will value it while you drive. Any equity goes straight to your deposit.
Do you service cars outside Nairobi and Mombasa?
Concierge members get collection and delivery across Kenya. Others can use our mobile service van, which visits the main cities every month.
